🤔 What Is mobile-mcp?

Think of mobile-mcp as a smart bridge between your computer's AI assistant and your smartphone. It lets an AI program (like a chatbot or automation tool) see and control your phone — just like a human would. You can ask it to open apps, tap buttons, read screenshots, fill forms, or collect information from websites.

Whether you're testing a new app, automating boring tasks, or gathering data from mobile sites, mobile-mcp does the heavy lifting for you.

✨ What Can You Do With It?

  • ⚙️ Automate anything — Let AI perform repetitive tasks on your phone (e.g., posting photos, clearing notifications, or testing app flows).
  • 📊 Scrape mobile data — Pull information from mobile-only websites or app screens automatically.
  • 📱 Works with any phone — iOS, Android, physical devices, emulators, and simulators are all supported.
  • 🧪 Testing made easy — Perfect for developers who want to check their apps without touching the phone manually.

🔧 Connecting Your Phone

📱 For Android (Real or Emulator):

Make sure "USB Debugging" is enabled on your device. In most cases, just plug your phone into your computer with a USB cable. The app will handle the rest. For emulators, just make sure it's running.

🍎 For iOS (Real or Simulator):

The tool automatically detects any connected iPhone or open simulator (like Xcode's Simulator). Nothing extra to set up from your side.

🏃 Running mobile-mcp

After you've downloaded the file and connected your phone, simply double-click the application. A small window will open on your screen — that's your control center. From there, you can start a conversation with your AI agent or let it take over.

For your first try, we recommend asking something simple like: "What's on my screen right now?" or "Open my email app." Watching it work is amazing!
